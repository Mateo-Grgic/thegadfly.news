What is this?

This is the bottom of the drawer—the non-mainline corner of The Gadfly. Comics nobody asked for, a zine, the odd manifesto, and a couple of "special" issues that we couldn't bring ourselves to number normally. It's a little weirder down here. That's on purpose.

The Gadfly Comic

Our ongoing comic, drawn by Hassan. The "real" one follows Ivan—grayscale with a little red, until he meets Harper and the colour bleeds into his life, right up until the betrayal turns the world grey again. The four-panel slapstick strips, meanwhile, stay exactly as lazily coloured as ever.
